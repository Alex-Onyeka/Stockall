Receipt Summary and Analysis
4.1 Purpose
The Receipt Summary page will provide an aggregated overview of the shop's receipts and transaction performance.

It should help users understand their sales activity, revenue generated, profit earned, customer purchasing behaviour, staff performance, departmental performance, payment channel distribution, and transaction trends.

The summary must use the same receipt data, financial calculations, transaction classifications, and business-day date filtering conventions as the Main Receipt Table.

All statistics and rankings must respect the selected shop and applicable reporting period.

Invoice payments and order deliveries must be handled according to their actual transaction roles to prevent duplicate counting of sales revenue.

4.2 Summary Statistics
The page must display the following summary statistics.

Statistic	Description
Total Receipts	Total number of qualifying receipt records within the selected reporting period.
Total Sales Receipts	Number of receipts classified as Sales.
Total Invoice Payments	Number of receipts classified as Invoice Payment.
Total Order Deliveries	Number of receipts classified as Order Delivery.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue for the selected period, using Stockall's existing financial reporting conventions.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate realized profit or loss from qualifying transactions.
Average Receipt Value	Average value of qualifying sales receipts.
Total Discounts	Aggregate discounts applied to qualifying sales receipts.
Total VAT	Aggregate VAT associated with qualifying sales receipts.
Total Items Sold	Total quantity of individual units sold across qualifying sales receipts, following Stockall's quantity rules.
Total Customers	Number of distinct customers associated with qualifying receipts.
Total Staff Involved	Number of distinct staff members associated with qualifying receipts.
Cash Transactions	Number of receipts classified under Cash.
Bank Transactions	Number of receipts classified under Bank.
Customer Account Transactions	Number of receipts classified under Customer Account.
Split Transactions	Number of receipts involving multiple payment channels.
4.2.1 Financial Calculation Rules
The developer must reuse Stockall's established financial calculations wherever possible.

Total Revenue: Sum the recognized sales revenue from qualifying sales transactions.

Total Profit/Loss: Aggregate the applicable receipt-level or item-level profit/loss values without double-counting.

Average Receipt Value: Total qualifying sales revenue divided by the number of qualifying Sales receipts.

Total Discounts: Sum the actual applicable discounts without double-counting percentage and fixed discounts.

Total VAT: Sum the applicable VAT amounts associated with qualifying sales receipts.

If there are no qualifying Sales receipts, Average Receipt Value must display zero or the application's standard empty-value representation without causing division by zero.

Invoice Payment receipts must not automatically count as additional sales revenue because they may represent payments against sales already recognized.

Order Delivery receipts must be treated according to Stockall's existing transaction model rather than automatically counted as new revenue.

4.2.2 Total Items Sold
The Total Items Sold statistic must represent the total quantity of individual units sold, not merely the number of product lines.

For example, a receipt containing 2 bottles and 3 bags contributes 5 items.

Use the existing TempProductSaleRecord quantity fields and Stockall's group-unit and dimensional inventory conventions.

Receipts with missing historical item details must be handled safely without inventing quantities.

4.2.3 Customer and Staff Counts
Count distinct customers and staff members rather than counting their receipt appearances.

Use stable customer and staff identifiers where available. Handle walk-in customers, missing customer IDs, and missing staff IDs consistently with the existing application.

Do not count an anonymous customer as a distinct registered customer merely because the receipt lacks a customer identifier.

4.3 Top 3 Customers
The page must display a table containing the three best-performing customers for the selected reporting period.

Rank customers primarily by total recognized sales revenue.

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential ranking position.
Customer	Customer name.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying Sales receipts associated with the customer.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue generated by the customer.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate profit or loss generated by the customer's qualifying sales.
Average Receipt Value	Average value of the customer's qualifying Sales receipts.
4.3.1 Customer Ranking Rules
Rank customers by Total Revenue in descending order.

Use the same financial calculation as the Main Receipt Table and Receipt Summary statistics.

Exclude Invoice Payment and Order Delivery records from the receipt count and revenue calculation unless Stockall's existing transaction model explicitly defines otherwise.

Handle anonymous or unregistered customers separately rather than merging unrelated transactions into a single customer.

Use a deterministic secondary sort when two customers have equal revenue.

If fewer than three eligible customers exist, display the available results.

4.4 Least 3 Performing Customers
The page must display the three least-performing eligible customers using the same revenue metric and reporting period as the Top 3 Customers section.

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential ranking position.
Customer	Customer name.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying Sales receipts.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate profit or loss.
Customers with no qualifying sales during the selected period should not be included in the ranking.

Apply a consistent tie-breaking rule and avoid misleading results caused by missing historical financial data.

4.5 Top 3 Performing Staff
The page must display the three staff members who generated the highest recognized sales revenue during the selected reporting period.

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential ranking position.
Staff	Staff member's name.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying Sales receipts handled by the staff member.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue associated with the staff member.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate profit or loss from the staff member's qualifying sales.
Average Receipt Value	Average value of the staff member's qualifying Sales receipts.
4.5.1 Staff Ranking Rules
Rank staff by Total Revenue in descending order.

Use the receipt's assigned staff identity, not the current logged-in user.

Exclude invoice payments and order deliveries from new-sales performance unless the existing business logic explicitly treats them as new sales.

Handle receipts without an assigned staff member separately.

Avoid counting the same receipt more than once.

4.7 Department Summary Table
The page must contain a Department Summary table when Department Management is enabled.

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential row number.
Department	Department name.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying Sales receipts associated with the department.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate profit or loss.
Average Receipt Value	Average qualifying receipt value.
Percentage of Revenue	Department revenue as a percentage of total qualifying shop revenue.
Items or receipts without a department assignment must be grouped under:

Others

4.7.1 Department Aggregation Rules
Use the receipt's existing department association.

Count qualifying receipts rather than sold item quantities.

Do not count the same receipt multiple times because it contains multiple products.

Use consistent receipt-level department attribution.

If the application allows a receipt to contain products from multiple departments, inspect the existing data model and determine whether department attribution belongs to the receipt or its individual sold lines. Do not arbitrarily assign the full receipt revenue to multiple departments.

Handle missing department assignments as Others.

Avoid division by zero when calculating the percentage of revenue.

4.7.2 Department Table Interaction
Each department row must be clickable.

When clicked, navigate to the Main Receipt Table and apply the selected department filter.

Clicking Others must display receipts without a department assignment.

The department filter must work with the existing search, other filters, sorting, and pagination.

The entire Department Summary section must be hidden when:

shop(context).manageDepartments == false

4.10 Payment Channel Summary
The page must contain a Payment Channel Summary section showing how customers pay for purchases.

The supported channels are:

Cash

Bank

Customer Account

Split

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential row number.
Channel	Payment channel.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying receipts classified under the channel.
Transaction Value	Applicable payment value associated with the channel.
Percentage of Transactions	Channel receipt count as a percentage of qualifying receipts.
Percentage of Value	Channel transaction value as a percentage of the applicable total.
4.10.1 Channel Classification Rules
Use the same payment-channel classification logic as the Main Receipt Table.

Inspect paymentMethod, cashAlt, bank, and customerAccount to establish the correct classification.

A Split receipt should be counted once in the Split receipt count, not once for each underlying payment method.

For transaction values, distinguish between:

Receipt-level sales value.

Cash collected.

Bank payments collected.

Customer-account amounts.

Payments allocated across multiple channels.

Do not assign the entire receipt value to every channel involved in a split payment.

If channel-level payment allocations are available, use those allocations to calculate channel transaction values. If they are not available or cannot be reliably reconstructed, do not fabricate them; use a clearly defined alternative or omit the unsupported value.

Avoid double-counting invoice payments as new sales revenue.

4.11 Transaction Type Summary
The page must display a summary of receipts grouped by transaction type.

The existing salesTypeIndex determines the type:

1 = Sales

2 = Invoice Payment

3 = Order Delivery

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential row number.
Transaction Type	Sales, Invoice Payment, or Order Delivery.
Receipt Count	Number of receipts of that type.
Total Transaction Value	Applicable value associated with the transaction type.
Total Profit/Loss	Applicable profit or loss, where meaningful and supported.
Keep transaction counts separate from recognized sales revenue.

Invoice Payment values should represent payments recorded, not additional sales revenue. Order Delivery values should follow the existing order-delivery model.

Unknown transaction types must be handled explicitly.

4.12 Revenue and Profit Trends
The page should include a time-series chart showing revenue and profit/loss over the selected reporting period.

The chart should support suitable time intervals based on the selected period, such as:

Daily.

Weekly.

Monthly.

Use Stockall's existing reporting date and business-day conventions.

Requirements:

Revenue must use the established recognized-sales-revenue calculation.

Profit/loss must use the existing financial calculation.

Invoice payments must not inflate sales revenue.

Date grouping must respect the shop's configured business-day closing time.

Empty periods must be handled consistently.

If there is insufficient historical data, display an appropriate empty state.

Where supported by the existing charting components, users should be able to distinguish revenue from profit/loss clearly.

4.13 Receipt Activity Trends
The page should display a chart or summary showing the number of qualifying receipts created over the selected reporting period.

Users should be able to identify periods with high and low transaction activity.

Where appropriate, separate the counts into:

Sales.

Invoice Payments.

Order Deliveries.

Do not combine these types in a way that makes receipt volume appear to represent the number of new sales.

The chart must follow the same date filtering and business-day logic as the Main Receipt Table.

4.14 Sub-Staff Summary
When Manage Bulk Sale is enabled, the page should contain a Sub-Staff Summary section.

Suggested columns:

Column	Description
Index Number	Sequential row number.
Sub-Staff	Sub-staff member's name.
Receipt Count	Number of qualifying Sales receipts attributed to the sub-staff member.
Total Revenue	Total recognized sales revenue associated with those receipts.
Total Profit/Loss	Aggregate profit or loss.
Use the existing subStaffUuid and subStaffName fields.

Handle receipts without an assigned sub-staff member consistently.

The entire Sub-Staff Summary section must be hidden when Manage Bulk Sale is disabled.

4.15 Date Range and Filtering
The Receipt Summary page must support the existing reporting date-range controls.

All summary statistics, tables, rankings, and charts must respond consistently to the selected reporting period.

Where the existing report architecture supports them, provide filters for:

Staff.

Customer.

Department, when Department Management is enabled.

Sub-Staff, when Manage Bulk Sale is enabled.

Payment Channel.

Transaction Type.

The date range and active filters must apply consistently across all sections.

The summary must also respect the selected shop and its data-access rules.

4.15.1 Business-Day Boundaries
Use Stockall's existing business-day closing-time functionality.

When no custom closing time is configured, use ordinary calendar-day boundaries.

When a custom closing time is configured, use the existing business-day boundary calculations.

Do not introduce an additional timezone offset or independently recalculate business-day boundaries in individual summary sections.

4.16 Data Integrity and Performance
Reuse the Main Receipt Table's financial calculations and receipt classifications.

Avoid duplicating revenue, profit, channel, and date-filtering logic.

Aggregate receipt-level metrics without counting the same receipt multiple times.

Use stable customer, staff, department, and sub-staff identifiers where available.

Handle null values and incomplete historical receipt records safely.

Avoid treating unavailable historical profit as zero.

Do not modify stored receipt transactions merely to generate summaries.

Avoid repeatedly recalculating the same receipt-level metrics during widget rebuilds.

Use the existing caching, state-management, and pagination conventions where appropriate.

Ensure all summary calculations use the correct selected shop.

4.17 UI and Interaction
Follow Stockall's existing UI, typography, colour palette, responsive layout, and reporting conventions.

Use summary cards for key statistics, tables for rankings and breakdowns, and charts for trends where appropriate.

Clickable summary rows must navigate to the Main Receipt Table with the corresponding filter applied, using the existing navigation and state-management architecture.

When a filter is applied through navigation, the Main Receipt Table must retain the filter until the user clears it or resets the filters.

Do not redesign unrelated pages or introduce unnecessary dependencies.

4.18 Testing and Verification
After implementation, verify:

All summary statistics use the correct receipt types and financial calculations.

Top and least-performing customers are ranked correctly.

Top and least-performing staff are ranked correctly.

Department revenue and profit are not duplicated across departments.

Customer and staff receipt counts count each qualifying receipt only once.

Payment-channel counts and values handle Split payments correctly.

Invoice Payments do not inflate recognized sales revenue.

Order Deliveries follow the existing transaction model.

Sub-Staff sections appear only when Manage Bulk Sale is enabled.

Department sections appear only when Department Management is enabled.

All sections respond to the selected date range and active filters.

Date grouping respects the configured business-day closing time.

Empty datasets, null values, and missing historical item details do not cause crashes or misleading financial results.

Clicking a department, customer, staff member, or sub-staff member applies the appropriate Main Receipt Table filter.

Revenue and profit trends match the corresponding aggregate statistics for the same reporting period.

At completion, provide a report of the files changed, the calculations reused, the functionality implemented, the tests performed, and any limitations caused by missing historical data.

Do not modify unrelated functionality or sensitive project configuration files unless genuinely necessary. If such a change is required, explain why before proceeding.

The final implementation must provide a reliable overview of Stockall's receipt activity without compromising the accuracy of its financial reports.
